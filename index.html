The government angle

It's a door, not a customer. No grant, no state buyer, no incentive scheme was discussed.

How Warren frames it

  • China: government stood up purpose-built "robot trainer" facilities, thousands of workers each. That's his gold standard.
  • Argentina: scaling through "politically connected billionaire types" who sell it to government as AI-future + local-economy uplift — workers earn a layer on top of existing pay.
  • Everyone's racing. "All the governments are racing to get as much data through as possible."

Why Thailand came up

Warren remembered Jarred's "crypto government" contacts (he first said Philippines; corrected to Thailand). Jarred: "Pat is the one who developed all these relationships — he still lives in Bangkok and maintains them."

What "government" has to deliver
  • Access to facility owners — CP-Group-tier agri and food conglomerates and their supply chains.
  • Political cover — a foreign company filming Thai workers at scale gets stopped by one mid-level official unless someone senior is sponsoring it.
  • A national-pride story — the Argentina template: local jobs, local AI narrative.

Red flag: Yida Gao

Pat: "Be careful with 'Eden' — people who worked at his fund all quit within three months. They were an investor of mine." Fieldy also rendered Yida as "Yita." Same person.

Public record

  • SEC v. Yida Gao & Shima Capital Management (N.D. Cal., filed Nov 25 2025; release Dec 3 2025): fraud — "false and misleading statements in raising more than $169.9 million."
  • Fund I deck claimed a 90× return on a prior deal that was actually 2.8×. BitClout SPV: resold tokens to his own investors at a markup, kept $1.9M undisclosed.
  • Settled: permanent injunction, $3.92M disgorgement; officer-and-director bar and penalties still pending. Parallel criminal case United States v. Yida Gao unsealed the same day.
  • Fortune, June 2024: CTO, head of research and chief of staff all left; Gao later stepped down and wound Shima down.

Why it matters here

  • Warren wants a tier-one non-crypto lead. A co-founder under SEC settlement with a live criminal case is the first thing that lead's counsel will find.
  • Every lab buying data runs vendor diligence. Same result.
  • Pat on the call: "he's not really super involved in this anyway — it's mostly just Warren." That may be true; it needs to be on paper.

Ask Warren directly: what is Yida's role, title, and cap-table position at Grip, and does the deck disclose the SEC matter?
