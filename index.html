03

The government angle

It's a door, not a customer. No grant, no state buyer, no incentive scheme was discussed.

How Warren frames it

  • China: government stood up purpose-built "robot trainer" facilities, thousands of workers each. That's his gold standard.
  • Argentina: scaling through "politically connected billionaire types" who sell it to government as AI-future + local-economy uplift — workers earn a layer on top of existing pay.
  • Everyone's racing. "All the governments are racing to get as much data through as possible."

Why Thailand came up

Warren remembered Jarred's "crypto government" contacts (he first said Philippines; corrected to Thailand). Jarred: "Pat is the one who developed all these relationships — he still lives in Bangkok and maintains them."

What "government" has to deliver
  • Access to facility owners — CP-Group-tier agri and food conglomerates and their supply chains.
  • Political cover — a foreign company filming Thai workers at scale gets stopped by one mid-level official unless someone senior is sponsoring it.
  • A national-pride story — the Argentina template: local jobs, local AI narrative.
